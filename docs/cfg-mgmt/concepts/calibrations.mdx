Calibrations flow one way, from the device to Miru. Editing calibrations is not supported.

## Properties

<ParamField path="device" type="Device">
  <ImmutableBadge />

  The [device](/concepts/devices/overview) that reported the calibration.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="component" type="Component">
  <ImmutableBadge />

  The [component](/cfg-mgmt/concepts/components) that was calibrated.

  Examples: `kuka-arm`, `realsense-d435`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="role" type="Role">
  <ImmutableBadge /> {' '} <NullableBadge />

  The [role](/cfg-mgmt/concepts/components#param-roles) the calibrated unit fills on the device.

  Examples: `left`, `front-left`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="aspect" type="Aspect">
  <ImmutableBadge /> {' '} <NullableBadge />

  Examples: `pose`, `intrinsics`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="serial number" type="string">
  <ImmutableBadge /> {' '} <NullableBadge />

  The serial number of the calibrated unit, not of the device. At most 255 characters.

  Example: `SN-1234`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="reported by" type="string">
  <ImmutableBadge /> {' '} <NullableBadge />

  The person who reported the calibration, exactly as the device named them. At most 255 characters.

  Example: `Jane Doe`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="format" type="enum">
  <ImmutableBadge />

  The file format of the calibration's content. Miru checks that `json`, `jsonc`, `yaml`, `xml` and `csv` content parses.

  Allowed values:
  - `json`
  - `yaml`
  - `jsonc`
  - `xml`
  - `csv`
  - `text`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="content" type="string">
  <ImmutableBadge />

  The calibration result, exactly as the device reported it. At most 1 MiB.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="reported at" type="datetime">
  <ImmutableBadge />

  When the device reported the calibration. A device that was offline uploads its calibrations later, so this can be well before Miru received them.
</ParamField>

## History

Calibrations are append-only. Every report adds a new calibration, even if its content matches an earlier one, and calibrations are never edited or deleted. They are only removed along with their device.

A device keeps a separate history for each component, role and aspect it reports. The most recently reported calibration in a history is that unit's current calibration.

## Report a calibration

<PlatformUnsupportedBadge />

Calibrations can only be created on-device using the Miru Agent. Visit the [report calibrations](/cfg-mgmt/report-calibrations) guide for more information.

## View calibrations

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="calibrations/list" />

To view a device's calibrations, open the device and navigate to the **Calibrations** page on the left.
