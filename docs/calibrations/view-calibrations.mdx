Each device's calibrations are accessible by clicking the device on the [Devices page](https://app.mirurobotics.com/devices) and selecting the **Calibrations** tab. Calibrations are read-only: you can view them, but not create, edit or delete them.

## Current calibrations

The **Calibrations** tab lists the device's current calibration for each [component](/calibrations/concepts/components), role and aspect it has reported: the newest one by the time it was reported.

## Filter calibrations

To narrow the list, filter by component, then by one of that component's roles or aspects. To find the calibrations of one physical unit, search by its serial number.

<Frame>
  [PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of the component, role and aspect filters and the serial number search on the Calibrations tab]
</Frame>

## View a calibration

To view a calibration, select it from the list. A panel opens with its content and details, including its serial number, the [deployment](/concepts/deployments/overview) the device was on, and when it was reported.

<Frame>
  [PLACEHOLDER: screenshot of the calibration panel showing its content and details]
</Frame>

## View calibration history

Each component, role and aspect on a device has its own history, newest first. Open a calibration's history to see every earlier calibration of that unit, with a text diff of what changed.
