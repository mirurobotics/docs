The [Devices page](https://app.mirurobotics.com/devices) lists every device in your
workspace. Use search, filters, and display options to find the devices you need.

## Search devices  <ViewerBadge />

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="devices/list" />

Click the search icon above the list, type, and press **Enter**. Search matches device
names by default; switch the field to **Description** to search descriptions instead.

<Frame>
  ![Device Search](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/devices/views/search.png)
</Frame>

## Filter devices  <ViewerBadge />

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="devices/list" />

Click the filter icon and filter by status, release, group, or agent version. You can
also find devices with no release, group, or agent version.

<Frame>
  ![Device Filter Menu](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/devices/views/filter-menu.png)
</Frame>

Selecting multiple values in the same filter widens the list: online and offline shows
devices that are online *or* offline. Adding another filter narrows it: online with
release `v2.0.0` shows devices that are online *and* on `v2.0.0`.

Active searches and filters appear as chips above the list. Click a chip's × to remove
it, or **Clear** to remove them all. Filters are saved in the page's URL, so you can
bookmark or share a filtered list.

## Display options  <ViewerBadge />

Click the display icon to group the list by status, release, group, or agent version,
sort it by name or status, switch to compact rows, or show and hide columns.

<Frame>
  ![Device Display Options](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/devices/views/display-menu.png)
</Frame>
