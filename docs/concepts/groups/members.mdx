A group's members are the people who can work on its devices. That includes owners,
admins, workspace operators and provisioners, and anyone with a role on the group or a
group above it. They're listed in the **Members** card of the group's details pane on
the [Groups page](https://app.mirurobotics.com/groups).

## Add members  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformUnsupportedBadge />

To add members, click the add members icon on the **Members** card.

<Frame>
  ![Group Members Card](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/members/members-card.png)
</Frame>

A dialog will appear—search by name, select the people you want to add, and click
**Add**. New members join with the [operator](/admin/users/access-control#operator) role.

## Edit a member's access  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformUnsupportedBadge />

To edit a member's access, click the role button next to them and check the roles you
want: **Manager**, **Operator**, or **Provisioner**.

<Frame>
  ![Edit Member Menu](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/members/edit-menu.png)
</Frame>

Roles from the member's workspace role or a parent group are locked. Hover over one to
see where it comes from. Owners and admins show their user type instead of roles.

To see all of a member's access, click their name or select **Manage access**. A panel
will appear with their workspace roles and every group they have a role on. To change a
role they inherit from a parent group, change it on that group in the panel.

<Frame>
  ![Member Access Panel](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/members/access-panel.png)
</Frame>

## Remove a member  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformUnsupportedBadge />

To remove a member, click the role button next to them and select **Remove access**.
This removes only the roles given on this group.

<Frame>
  ![Remove Member Menu](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/members/remove-menu.png)
</Frame>
