## View a group

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="groups/get" />

To view a group, navigate to the [Groups page](https://app.mirurobotics.com/groups) and
click the group in the tree. The page has three panes:

- **Tree:** Navigate your groups like a file directory
- **Devices:** View, filter, and organize the selected group's
  [devices](#view-devices-in-a-group)
- **Details:** The group's properties and [members](/concepts/groups/members)

<Frame>
  ![Group Page](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/manage/page.png)
</Frame>

## View devices in a group  <ViewerBadge />

The middle pane lists the devices assigned to the group. Devices in its subgroups aren't
included; open a subgroup to see them.

The list works like the one on the Devices page: you can
[search, filter, and save views](/concepts/devices/views) and use
[bulk actions](/concepts/devices/manage#bulk-actions). Views saved here appear only in
this group.

To create a device in the group, click **+ Device** in the top right.

## Create a group  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="groups/create" />

Admins can create a top-level group by clicking the plus **(+)** at the top of the tree.
To create a subgroup, hover over the parent group and click its plus **(+)**.

<Frame>
  ![Create Group](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/manage/create-group.png)
</Frame>

A dialog will appear—enter a name and click **Create**.

## Rename a group  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="groups/update" />

To rename a group, click the ellipses **(...)** on the group in the tree, or right-click
it, and hit **Rename**.

<Frame>
  ![Rename Group Menu](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/manage/rename-menu.png)
</Frame>

A dialog will appear—enter the new name and click **Rename**.

## Move a group  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformApiBadge endpoint="groups/move" />

To move a group, click the ellipses **(...)** on the group in the tree, or right-click
it, and hit **Move**. Its subgroups and devices move with it.

<Frame>
  ![Move Group Menu](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/manage/move-menu.png)
</Frame>

A dialog will appear—select the new parent group and click **Move**. To move the group
to the top level, click the selected group again to clear it. Only admins can move a
group to the top level.

<Frame>
  ![Move Group Dialog](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/manage/move-dialog.png)
</Frame>

## Delete a group  <AdminBadge />  <GroupManagerBadge />

<PlatformUnsupportedBadge />

A group can only be deleted if it has no subgroups and no assigned devices. If you need to delete a group that still contains devices or subgroups, first move or reassign its contents.

Deleting a group also removes its members' roles on it and the device views saved on it.

To delete a group, click the ellipses **(...)** on the group in the tree, or right-click
it, and hit **Delete**.

<Frame>
  ![Delete Group Menu](https://assets.mirurobotics.com/docs/v04/images/groups/manage/delete-menu.png)
</Frame>

A dialog will appear—click **Delete** to confirm.
